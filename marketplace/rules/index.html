Rules and safe buying

Plain-language guide. It is not legal advice; you are responsible for following the law.

How it works

  • Sellers submit an item with photos. We review it before it appears. A listing runs for 90 days.
  • Buyers send an enquiry. We introduce buyer and seller; contact details are shared only with that one person.
  • The sale happens between buyer and seller. Veda Heritage does not hold stock, take payments, ship goods, or guarantee authenticity, quality or price.
  • Listings are free for now. We may decline or remove any listing at our discretion.

What is not allowed

Antiquities
Items 100 years old or more, and manuscripts 75 years or more. Selling and exporting these is regulated in India.
Wildlife and animal products
Ivory, tusk, horn, bone, shell, coral, feathers, fur, skins and anything from protected species.
Regulated timber and plants
Sandalwood, rosewood and similar timber in raw or restricted form. Live plants and seeds are not accepted for now.
Religious idols and temple property
Idols, ritual items or fittings taken from temples or places of worship, or of unclear origin.
Medicines and health products
Ayurvedic or other medicines, oils sold with health claims, tablets, powders and supplements.
Weapons and hazardous goods
Weapons, ammunition, fireworks, chemicals.
Counterfeits and false claims
Imitations sold as protected crafts, or goods described as "genuine", "certified" or "GI" without proof.
Stolen or disputed goods
Anything you do not have the right to sell.
Precious metals and stones
Gold, silver, gemstones and jewellery: hallmarking and valuation rules apply.

If you are unsure whether an item is allowed, ask us before listing. Laws that may apply include the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, the Wildlife (Protection) Act and the Drugs and Cosmetics Act.

Buying safely

  • Inspect in person where you can, and ask for extra photos.
  • Pay only when you receive the item. Never pay in advance, by gift card, or by crypto.
  • Treat claims of age, origin or authenticity as the seller's opinion unless you have independent proof.
  • Be careful with shipping long distances or abroad: ask a courier about export rules first.

Your data

We collect only what the form asks for. Seller contact details and photos are stored privately and are deleted if a listing is not approved or is removed. Buyer details are deleted once we have made the introduction. Published listings show the seller's first name only. To ask for your data to be removed, use the contact form.
