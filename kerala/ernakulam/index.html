Kerala · Central Kerala

Ernakulam

Kerala's commercial capital and a multi-faith port city

Ernakulam: Kochi's colonial quarter, Jew Town, a major port, the Kochi-Muziris Biennale, Hill Palace and the folklore museum.

Headquarters: Kochi

A brief history

Kochi became prominent after a great flood of 1341 opened the harbour. Portuguese, Dutch and British ruled in turn, and Jewish, Arab and Chinese traders shaped its cosmopolitan culture.

  1. 1341

    Periyar flood opens Kochi harbour.

  2. 1503

    Portuguese build Fort Immanuel.

  3. 2012

    First Kochi-Muziris Biennale is held.

What makes Ernakulam unique

  • Port city with Portuguese, Dutch and British heritage
  • Mattancherry Palace and Jewish quarter
  • Chendamangalam handloom
  • Kochi-Muziris Biennale

Best time to visit

November to February

Pleasant weather for walking and art events.

Avoid: June–August (monsoon)

What to look for

  • Fort Kochi and fishing nets
  • Paradesi Synagogue
  • Hill Palace Museum
  • Kerala Folklore Museum

Highlights

  • Fort Kochi
  • Mattancherry
  • Hill Palace
  • Bolgatty

Festivals & events

  • Kochi-Muziris Biennale (Dec–Mar, biennial)
  • Thripunithura Athachamayam (Aug–Sep)

Dates change yearly; confirm before travelling.

Crafts & local products: Handloom, Metal mirrors, spices.
